About the model

Digital reconstruction

Dimensions follow the museum record. The shapes and fittings were reconstructed from photographs and Alex Whittaker’s model-aircraft drawing. The airframe view interprets the internal structure. The wing-warping deflection is approximate.

The landscape and its story stops are fictional. Flight dynamics and engine audio are simplified for exploring and landing.
